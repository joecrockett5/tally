# Third-party notices

Tally itself is released under the [MIT License](LICENSE). It bundles, or runs
alongside, the third-party work listed here. Licences were checked against the
packages actually installed for this release: `package.json`, `.nuspec` and licence
files for libraries, and `/usr/share/doc/*/copyright` or `apk list` for operating
system packages.

Tally ships **no third-party fonts, icons or images**. It uses the system font stack,
and its icons and logo are generated by [`tools/icons/make_icons.py`](tools/icons/make_icons.py)
in this repository. Profile avatars are emoji drawn by the viewer's own device.
